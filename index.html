Quick answers for planning your first visit with Beauty by Kyrin.

Where is Beauty by Kyrin located?+

Beauty by Kyrin is inside Venus Salon at 8665 W Flamingo Rd, Las Vegas, NV 89147.

What hair services does Kyrin offer?+

Kyrin offers custom cuts, dimensional color, highlights, balayage, extensions, blowouts, keratin services, deep conditioning, and scalp care.

How should I prepare for a color appointment?+

Bring inspiration photos and share your recent color history. Kyrin will use the consultation to shape a realistic plan for your hair.

How is extension pricing determined?+

Extension pricing depends on your starting hair, desired length and volume, hair quantity, and method. A consultation provides an accurate quote.
