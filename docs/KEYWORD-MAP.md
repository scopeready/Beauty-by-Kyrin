## 1. THE RULE THIS MAP ENFORCES

**One primary intent per page. No two pages share a primary keyword.**

The three places that rule was at risk, and how each was resolved:

| Risk | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Home vs the service-area page both wanting local discovery | Home owns **hair stylist las vegas** (whole-offer, brand). The service-area page owns **hair salon spring valley las vegas** (neighborhood). "Near me" sits on the service-area page, not home. |
| Balayage vs color & highlights | Balayage owns freehand technique. Color & highlights owns foils, all-over color, gray blending and root work. Neither page uses the other's primary term in a title or H1. |
| Service pages vs journal posts | Service pages hold **commercial** intent ("balayage las vegas"). Posts hold **informational** intent ("does balayage damage hair"). The post links up to the service page; the service page links down to the post. |

**Priority tiers** — build in this order:
**Tier 1** (attainable, high intent): `/services/balayage`, `/las-vegas-hair-stylist`, `/new-guests`, `/services/hair-treatments`
**Tier 2** (attainable with content depth): `/services/color-and-highlights`, `/services/haircuts-and-styling`, `/faq`, journal comparison posts
**Tier 3** (contested; win on long tail only): `/services/hair-extensions`, `/`, `/services`
**Support** (convert, do not rank): `/about`, `/portfolio`, `/journal`

> **Before any of this:** her Google Business Profile outranks the whole site for "near me" and Maps intent. Confirm she has her own GBP at the Venus Salon address, distinct from the salon's.

## 4. INTERNAL LINKING

Links carry intent between the two halves of the map. Every journal post links **up** to its service page; every service page links **down** to the posts that answer its objections.

| From | To | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `/services/balayage` | `/journal/how-often-balayage-touch-up`, `/journal/does-balayage-damage-hair`, `/journal/balayage-vs-highlights` | Answers the maintenance and damage objections that stop a booking |

| `/services/hair-extensions` | `/journal/tape-in-vs-hand-tied-extensions`, `/journal/hair-extensions-consultation` | Method choice and the consultation gate |
| `/services/hair-treatments` | `/journal/olaplex-vs-k18`, `/journal/las-vegas-climate-and-your-hair` | The two highest-value informational posts on the site |
| `/journal/las-vegas-climate-and-your-hair` | `/services/hair-treatments`, `/las-vegas-hair-stylist` | Converts local informational traffic |
| `/new-guests` | `/faq`, `/portfolio`, booking | The cautious-first-timer path |
| `/las-vegas-hair-stylist` | all five service pages | Distributes local authority into commercial pages |
| Every page | booking / contact | A solo stylist's only conversion |

**Do not** link every page to every other page. The map above is deliberately directional: authority should pool in Tier 1 pages.

## 5. WHAT THIS MAP DELIBERATELY EXCLUDES
